CT78.A10 · Synth

A toy synthesizer floating in the dark, in the browser (three.js / WebGL), played from MIDI, with a doll at the keys. Art project.

20 white and 14 black keys (C3–A5). It starts playing songs by itself (a player piano): Silent Night, Joy to the World (four verses) and We Wish You a Merry Christmas, each on its own instrument; after the last there is a 5 s pause and it starts again. The centre caroler doll from CT68 (no candle) plays along, her right hand the tune and her left hand the bass: palms down, fingertips landing on the keys as they go down, looking down at her hands, mouthing the words of the carols. When a key goes down it glows red; nothing else changes its glow. The toy's display changes colour and picture with the instrument. Three small clear-glass vacuum tubes with a glowing orange filament; eight jacks with lights. The Doll group in the right-hand column has the controls of the original CT68 dolls (head, eyes, mouth, blink, sway, spin, Sing, Talk). The panel includes Record (an MP4 movie with sound).

Open index.html in Chrome or Edge; the first line of the Info panel says CT78.A10.Synth. P stops / starts play mode, H hides the panel, V changes the view, ? help. CT78.A10.Synth.md has the details; ISSUES.md the open issues.
